What I do

My main interests are related to cellular metabolism in health and disease, mitochondrial biology and mitochondrially targeted compounds, cancer metabolism and reliable and traceable research methods including Bayesian data analysis in R.

I very much enjoy teaching and as an educator I am interested in new teaching approaches and methods. I post my lecture recordings on my YouTube channel.
